## 👨‍💻 About Me

<img align="right" width="180" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/innng/innng/master/assets/kyubey.gif" alt="Animated character"/>

Hello! I'm **Pravyanth Marreddy**, a B.Tech student specializing in **Robotics and Automation** at Vignan's Foundation for Science, Technology & Research.

I'm passionate about the intersection of **robotics, electronics, embedded programming, and artificial intelligence**. My goal is to turn theoretical knowledge into working prototypes and intelligent engineering solutions.

- 🤖 Exploring robotics, automation, and motion control.
- 🔌 Building with Arduino, sensors, and electronic components.
- 💻 Developing programming skills in C and Python.
- 🧠 Experimenting with AI agents, APIs, and RAG fundamentals.
- 🛠️ Learning through hands-on projects and hardware troubleshooting.
- 🚀 Working toward becoming a capable robotics and embedded systems engineer.

> *My philosophy: Understand the fundamentals. Build something real. Test it. Improve it.*
